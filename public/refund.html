Please read this No Refund Policy carefully before purchasing any online course, digital product, educational program, membership, or related service made available through the SkillCrust platform. By completing a purchase, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Policy.

At SkillCrust, we are committed to maintaining a transparent purchasing experience for all learners.

1. No Refunds

All purchases made through the SkillCrust platform are final, non-cancellable, and non-refundable.

Since SkillCrust provides digital educational products and services that are delivered electronically or made accessible shortly after successful payment, no refund, cancellation, exchange, partial refund, or credit request will be accepted once a transaction has been successfully completed, except where such rights are expressly required under applicable law.

2. Digital Products & Services

SkillCrust offers digital courses, educational programs, downloadable resources, memberships, mentoring services, and other digital learning solutions.

Due to the nature of these products and services, they cannot be returned or exchanged after purchase.

3. Purchase Confirmation

Before making any purchase, users are encouraged to carefully review:

  • Course or product description

By proceeding with payment, you confirm that you have independently reviewed the relevant information and voluntarily chosen to purchase the selected product or service.

4. Payment Processing

Payments made for products or services available through the SkillCrust platform are securely processed by our authorised payment partners.

A successful payment confirms your purchase of the selected digital product or service and does not create any entitlement to a refund except where required by applicable law.

5. Unauthorized or Technical Payment Issues

If you believe a payment has been processed due to an unauthorized transaction or a genuine technical issue, you should immediately contact our official support team.

Such requests will be reviewed solely for transaction verification and compliance purposes. Submission of a request does not constitute approval of any refund.

SkillCrust reserves the right to modify, revise, or update this No Refund Policy at any time without prior notice.

The latest version published on the website shall apply to all future purchases.
