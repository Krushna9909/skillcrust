Refund Policy

Placeholder — not final, and not yet confirmed internally. This draft reflects the current no-refunds assumption noted in the product specification (referral rewards are credited instantly, with no reversal mechanism, so a refund after purchase cannot claw back rewards already paid out to referrers). This needs sign-off from both a lawyer and the business before launch — see the "Open Flag for Confirmation" in the underlying spec.

Last updated: draft, not yet published.

1. Course purchases are final

All course purchases are final once your account and course access are confirmed. Because a portion of every purchase is credited instantly and irreversibly to referrers as part of SkillCrust's referral program, we are currently unable to offer refunds on completed purchases.

2. Failed payments

If a payment fails or is not confirmed, you will not be charged and no course access will be granted. You're welcome to try the purchase again.

3. Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests, once submitted and approved, are processed through our payout system and cannot be recalled. If a payout attempt fails on our end, the amount is returned to your wallet balance automatically and is not deducted from your account.

4. Account issues

If you believe you were charged in error or experienced a technical issue during purchase, contact us using the details SkillCrust will publish here once finalized, and we'll look into it on a case-by-case basis.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, including potentially introducing a limited refund window, before SkillCrust launches with real payments. Any change will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
