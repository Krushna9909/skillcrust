1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, state, and password. If you complete KYC for withdrawals, we additionally collect your Aadhaar number, PAN number, bank account details, and/or UPI ID.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to operate your account, process course purchases, calculate and credit referral rewards, process withdrawals, and communicate with you about your account.

3. Data security

Your Aadhaar number, PAN number, and bank account number are encrypted at rest and are never written to logs. These fields are masked everywhere in the product except at the moment you enter them and in the admin view used for verification. All traffic to SkillCrust is encrypted in transit.

4. Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Third-party services

Course payments and withdrawal payouts are currently processed through a mock/sandbox payment system during development. Once SkillCrust moves to real payments, this section will name the actual payment and payout providers used.

6. Your rights

You can review and update your name, email, phone number, state, and profile photo at any time from your account. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account, contact us using the details below.

7. Data retention

We retain account and transaction records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward, as required for financial recordkeeping.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details SkillCrust will publish here once finalized.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
